Frequently Asked Questions & Tips

How do I download YouTube Shorts & Facebook Reels?

Just click the Share button on any YouTube Short or Facebook Reel, copy the link, paste it into the box above, and click Fetch Video. The full resolution MP4 file without watermarks will be generated instantly.

How do I download Instagram Reels?

Ensure the Instagram Reel is from a public profile. Paste the link (e.g., https://www.instagram.com/reel/XYZ...) and click Fetch. For private accounts, Instagram blocks guest API access.

How do I download Kuaishou (快手 / Kwai) videos?

Paste either the kuaishou.com web URL or the mobile share link (v.kuaishou.com/... or kwai.com/...). Our multi-engine resolver parses the redirect and pulls the raw CDN .mp4 file without platform branding.

Can I run a local high-performance engine?

Yes! In the top-right Settings menu (), you can connect your own custom Cobalt instance or local yt-dlp backend to get unlimited unrestricted speed forever.
